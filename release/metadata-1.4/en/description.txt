KinetMinutes turns your meetings into structured notes — automatically, privately, and entirely on your Mac.

DETECT · RECORD · NOTES
Start a call in Zoom, Tencent Meeting, Teams, Feishu (Lark), DingTalk, Discord or Webex and KinetMinutes starts recording. When the meeting ends, local AI writes your notes: a clear summary, agreed decisions, and action items with owners. No "Save transcript" clicking, no uploading.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
• 100% on-device: Whisper transcription and AI summarization both run locally
• Zero network calls for your audio and notes — works fully offline
• Audio, transcripts and notes stay in your own library; delete everything anytime
• The choice for HR interviews, client negotiations, legal and strategy meetings

NOTES, NOT RAW TRANSCRIPTS
• Summary — the meeting in a few sentences
• Decisions — what was actually agreed
• Action items — tasks with owners and due dates
• Timeline — timestamped key moments, click to jump to the exact point
• Full transcript when you need every word

CHINESE-FIRST AI
Optimized for Mandarin + English code-switching, the gap global tools leave open. Whisper models download once and then run offline.

MEETING LIBRARY
Every meeting is searchable across notes and transcripts. Rename, tag by source app, export.

WHAT THE APP INCLUDES
• Unlimited recording and offline transcription — no monthly cap
• All four Whisper model sizes, from base (150 MB) to large-v3 (3 GB)
• The full meeting library: search, tags, Markdown / TXT / SRT export
• 5 AI-generated note sets per calendar month

KINETMINUTES PRO — OPTIONAL, ONE-TIME
Pro removes the monthly note limit: unlimited AI notes, same app, same local files. It is an in-app purchase, not a subscription, and there is no account to create. Everything you have already recorded keeps working whether or not you upgrade.

MENU BAR NATIVE
Lives in your menu bar. Launch-at-login optional. Native macOS design — no Electron, no browser engine.
