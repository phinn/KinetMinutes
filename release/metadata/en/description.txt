KinetMinutes turns your meetings into structured notes — automatically, privately, and entirely on your Mac.

DETECT · RECORD · NOTES
Start a call in Zoom, Tencent Meeting, Teams, Feishu (Lark), DingTalk, Discord or Webex and KinetMinutes starts recording. When the meeting ends, local AI generates your notes: a clear summary, agreed decisions, and action items with owners. No "Save transcript" clicking. No uploading.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
• 100% on-device: transcription (Whisper) and AI summarization run locally
• Zero network calls for your audio and notes — works fully offline
• Audio, transcripts and notes stay in your library; delete everything anytime
• Perfect for HR interviews, client negotiations, legal and strategy meetings

NOTES, NOT RAW TRANSCRIPTS

• Decisions — what was actually agreed
• Action items — tasks with owners and due dates
• Timeline — timestamped key moments, click to the exact point
• Full transcript when you need every word
