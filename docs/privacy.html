KinetMinutes

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 2026-09-19

Zero upload. By design.

KinetMinutes runs entirely on your Mac. Recording, transcription (Whisper) and AI summarization all happen locally. Your audio and notes never leave this computer — the app makes no server calls, has no accounts, and no analytics.

Data we collect

None. KinetMinutes does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Where your data lives

  • Audio recordings: ~/Library/Containers/com.kinet.minutes.app/Data/Library/Application Support/KinetMinutes/Audio/
  • Transcripts & notes: local SQLite database in the same folder
  • You can delete everything at any time via Privacy → Delete All Data, or uninstall the app

Model downloads

The only network activity is a one-time, user-initiated download of open-source Whisper model weights from huggingface.co (or a user-configured mirror). Your audio is never uploaded.

Third parties

None. No SDKs, no trackers, no ads.

Contact

Questions about privacy: phinn@kinet.app
